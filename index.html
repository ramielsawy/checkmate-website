Frequently Asked Questions

What is a hospitality integrity audit?
An integrity audit examines your operations for revenue leakage: unrecorded sales, POS manipulation, cash handling issues, staff consumption, and free-pouring. We use AI and transaction analysis to detect patterns that manual checks often miss.
Who do Checkmate Consultants work with?
We work with luxury hotels, restaurants, bars, and spas in the UAE and beyond—operators who value discretion, measurable results, and protecting both revenue and brand reputation.
How does AI improve your audits?
Our AI systems analyze transaction behaviors, anomaly clusters, and systemic inconsistencies across large datasets. This complements human expertise so we can spot patterns and risks that sampling alone cannot reliably detect.
Is the audit process confidential?
Yes. All audits are conducted under strict confidentiality protocols with minimal operational disruption. Your brand reputation is protected at every stage.
